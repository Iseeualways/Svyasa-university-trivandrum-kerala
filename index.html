S-VYASA University (Deemed-to-be University), Trivandrum, Kerala

Swami Vivekananda Yoga Anusandhana Samsthana, popularly known as S-VYASA University, is a UGC-approved Deemed-to-be University. As part of its commitment to academic excellence and holistic development, the university has established a new campus in Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala, popularly known as the S-VYASA Deemed-to-be University Trivandrum campus. Located in the Evergreen City of India, this expansion aims to extend the university's academic vision beyond geographical boundaries. Through a unique blend of traditional knowledge and modern teaching methodologies, the university strives to nurture students into skilled professionals and future leaders.
